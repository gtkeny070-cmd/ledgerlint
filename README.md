# LedgerLint

Free beta: check a bank CSV before importing transactions into QuickBooks Online. Map columns, review invalid dates and amounts, flag exact duplicate rows, and download a three-column CSV.

**Try it:** https://ledgerlint.runs-at.dev/

CSV processing happens in your browser. Keep your original export and review the result against your bank statement before importing. LedgerLint is independent of Intuit and cannot guarantee that QuickBooks accepts a file.

Feedback: ledgerlintt@gmail.com. Please do not send bank statements or unredacted transaction data.
